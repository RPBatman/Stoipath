Design rationale · Concept A · Canvas-maximal — what is different, trade-offs, what to look at, engine-contract map

The idea in one sentence

The slide is the interface. At rest the only chrome is two thin bars (identity above, status below) and one corner cluster; everything else — the case panel, the full tray, layers, compare, bookmarks, the keymap, the palette and the tools — is a transient that the expert summons with one key and dismisses with Esc, and every transient opens from the edge or at the pointer, never over the centre. Canvas share at rest is ≈ 93 % at 1920 × 1080 and ≈ 96 % at 3840 × 2160 (the baseline frame in the component sheet is ≈ 74 % / 84 %).

Key choices

  1. No rail, no dock; one corner cluster. The navigator (region F) absorbs what the rail and dock carried: the viewport rectangle and trail, the Tier-2 label image, the three slide thumbnails with viewed / partly / unviewed state and coverage, and a row of five summon tabs with their keys. The cluster is the only persistent object over the canvas and it is the discoverability layer: the keys are printed on it.
  2. One drawer slot. Every panel (Case I, Slides ], Layers ⇧H, Compare C, Marks ⇧B) opens in the same slot directly under the cluster, docked to the right edge, so the pathologist's eye learns one place. Only one panel is open at a time; Esc closes it. On tablet portrait and phone the slot becomes the bottom sheet (≤ 40 % height) the responsive strategy prescribes.
  3. Tools are momentary keys and a radial at the pointer. Single-letter keys select tools modelessly (map B); right-click, long-press, the Menu key or a Pencil squeeze opens a radial of the eight most-used tools around the pointer with the key printed on each wedge. The radial is a user-invoked transient GPU layer in the product, exempt from the central-zone rule like the pen-tip palette; it is the plan's right-click "here" menu drawn as a ring. The bottom bar on touch keeps five labelled 44 px targets so tablets are not radial-only.
  4. Command echo. Every dispatched command briefly prints its name under the identity line (› zoom-preset-20x). It is the visible twin of the proposed commandInvoked shell event, and it teaches the keymap by use: the expert sees what the key did; the novice sees what to search for in Ctrl+K.
  5. One registry drives everything. The keymap sheet, the palette, the radial, the bottom bar and the key handler are rendered from the same command array, as the plan's command bus intends (FR-V12). Rebinding a key would change all of them at once; the reserved dictation keys are shown struck through everywhere.
  6. Identity never moves. Accession, part/slide, synthetic patient line and worklist state stay in the fixed top bar; the identity line sits top-left of the canvas; the label image lives in the cluster (collapsible with L in Nav only, never in Dx — Design note D13). The product badge, the full label string and the NAV/DX indicator are outside every dismissible layer (P4).
  7. Honest pixels. The HUD magnification is derived from µm/px and says so; when the display cannot be attested it reads ~20× with Pitch unknown and Dx is refused with the fixed microcopy. Degraded states (cold 4G, offline, tile errors, slow translator, Tier 2) each have their named strip state; failed tiles keep the ancestor and are outlined, never blank.

Trade-offs, honestly

  • Discoverability is the risk and the summon tabs are the mitigation. A first-week user has to learn that panels exist behind five tiny tabs and a right-click. The tabs, the command echo and the palette are the three teaching devices; a formative study on the pilot lab's residents should measure time-to-first-panel against the baseline frame before this concept is chosen.
  • The cluster is wider than the §7 navigator (320 px desktop, 300 px laptop, 360 px 4K, 200 px tablet vs 240/320/180) because it also carries the tray and the tabs; net chrome is still a third of the baseline's rail + dock.
  • A drawer covers the right third of the canvas while open and the annotation list, layer rows and bookmarks share one slot, so comparing two panels needs two keystrokes rather than a glance. The baseline's persistent dock wins when a pathologist reads with the layer panel permanently open (AI-assisted screening).
  • Keyboard-first favours the companion-window sign-out. Inside the Hyperdrive iframe the controller binds no keys at all (principles §4.5 rule 5), so the embedded variant falls back to tray chips and a hand-off button; the concept's strengths do not apply there by design.
  • Right-click as a tool entry conflicts with nothing in the plan, but some pathologists rotate with a held right button in other viewers; the radial opens on release without drag, and middle-drag rotation stays as the plan has it.

What the judges should look at

  • At rest (1920 × 1080 and 3840 × 2160): cover the canvas with your hand — badge, label string, mode indicator, identity and the Rendering Complete string are all still there (P4/P5 test). Nothing crosses the central 50 % × 50 % (toggle the guide in the demo strip).
  • Press ?: the keymap names the active preset and the reserved dictation keys. Press Ctrl+K and type meas: the same registry, with slides and bookmarks mixed in.
  • Press ⇧H then turn the AI layer on: provenance tuple, Not validated for diagnostic use on this viewer, outline-first with heatmap on demand, accept / reject, and the in-viewport legend that appears bottom-right and jumps to a class on click. In Dx the layer is off by default.
  • Switch the Dx gate to no attestation: NAV, the refusal string in the strip, ~20× with Pitch unknown, and the Rendering Complete vocabulary disappears.
  • Switch the variant to Consult · iPad Safari at a 1024-wide window: purpose chip, Tier 2 · 60 Hz, slow-link chip, bottom bar, drawer as a sheet, no label image (Tier 0), Send preliminary note and Request consult in the case sheet.
  • Switch the product to Viewer (RUO): the LIS launch is refused with the fixed plate — the build boundary is a build, not a flag.
  • Press C then Open side-by-side: H&E beside CK AE1/AE3 with lock sync, active pane outlined, one shared magnification readout.

Design notes (conflicts flagged, not resolved here)

  • DN-A1 · Scale bar length. The brief (§10.1) asks for a 200 µm bar at 20× (0.50 µm/px); that is 400 CSS px, outside the 80–200 px rule of §5.2. This concept draws the honest 100 µm at 200 px and keeps 20× / 0.50 µm/px. The 200 µm label appears when the view is at 10× (1.0 µm/px). P8 (micrometres are the only truth) was given priority over literal compliance with the brief's example values.
  • DN-A2 · Measurement at 20×. A 2.3 mm focus does not fit a 20× field (≈ 960 µm wide at 1920 px), so at 20× the measurement line is partly visible and its label follows the visible end; press 3 (5×) to see it whole. The label is DOM in this mock and a GPU label in the product.
  • DN-A3 · Navigator width. 320 / 300 / 360 / 200 px (desktop / laptop / 4K / tablet) instead of §7's 240 / 320 / 180 because the cluster carries the tray and tabs; it still never enters the central 50 %.
  • DN-A4 · Radial menu. Principles §4.1 specifies a right-click context menu ("Bookmark here, Measure from here, Copy view link"). The radial presents the same commands plus the drawing tools as a ring; it dispatches on the command bus like every other invoker.
  • DN-A5 · Purpose chip on narrow bars. At 1024 px with the long consult string, the chip and the full label string cannot both fit beside the accession; this concept keeps the label string and accession (rules §2.5) and moves the purpose chip into the strip between 1041 and 1439 px of bar width; below that the label string is behind the badge and the chip returns to the bar; on phones (not a consult-by-chip surface) the purpose is read from the badge's About sheet. The chip's four strings are unchanged.
  • DN-A7 · Label string at exactly 1024 px. base.css hides the label string by a size-container query on the top bar's content box (viewport − 16 px padding), so at a 1024 px viewport it is already hidden; at 1024 the string (≈ 800 px at 12 px), badge, accession, mode and overflow would overflow the bar anyway. The §2.5 threshold should be restated as ≥ 1040 px of viewport, or the string allowed to wrap to a second line between 1024 and 1100 px. Flagged for the design-system owner.
  • DN-A6 · Sign-out in the strip. A small Request sign-out ⇧↵ button sits in the strip's right group in the sign-out variant so the once-per-case action is visible without opening the Case panel; the hand-off dialog remains the only route to signoutRequested.
