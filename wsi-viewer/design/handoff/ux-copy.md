## 0. Conventions

- **IDs** are `namespace.key`. Namespaces: `label` (product labelling), `bar` (top bar), `mode` (render mode and Dx gate), `strip` (status strip), `net` (network, tier, offline), `err` (errors, plates, banners), `empty`, `hud`, `nav` (navigator), `tool` (accessible names of tools), `cmd` (command registry labels, palette rows), `key` (keymap text), `clin` (clinical workflow), `res` (research workspace), `learn` (education), `dlg` (dialogs shared across builds), `toast`, `a11y` (live-region sentences that differ from the visual only by punctuation), `about`.
- **Placeholders** use ICU syntax: `{accession}`, `{slide}` (`A1-1`), `{stain}` (`H&E`, `CK AE1/AE3`), `{mag}` (`20×`), `{umpp}` (`0.50`), `{n}`, `{total}`, `{pct}`, `{display}` (`Example display DX-27`), `{days}`, `{mbps}`, `{p95}`, `{pack}`, `{date}`, `{labName}`, `{product}`, `{build}`, `{name}` (always a synthetic placeholder in mocks). Plurals use ICU `plural`.
- **Typography in strings.** Middle dot ` · ` separates metadata fields; an em dash ` — ` introduces a reason or consequence (`Soft — slow link`); `…` (U+2026) marks an in-progress state, never three periods; `×` (U+00D7) for magnification; `µm`, `mm`, `mm²` with a thin space before the unit in prose and a normal space in the HUD; `≥`, `≤`, `≈` as symbols; sentence case everywhere except the four product badges and the mode indicator (`RUO`, `LEARN`, `CLINICAL-IH`, `DX`, `NAV`), which are uppercase; QuPath class names verbatim (`Tumor`, `Region*`).
- **Numbers.** `Intl` formatting; magnification `20×` / `2.5×` / `1.25×` (trailing zeros trimmed); µm/px two decimals (`0.50 µm/px`); lengths in µm below 1 mm, mm above (`2.3 mm`); areas `1.9 mm²`; percentages with a space (`78 %`); times `09:41`, durations `06:12`; dates ISO (`2026-10-24`) in metadata, relative in status chips (`3 days ago`).
- **Shortcuts in text** are rendered from the command registry as key caps (`<kbd class="key">`), never typed into the sentence; the strings below show them in angle-free form (`⇧↵`, `Ctrl+K`) where the key is part of the sentence, and the accessible name spells them out (`shortcut Shift Enter`, `Control K`).
- **Synthetic content.** `AP-26-04417`, `DOE, JANE · 1958`, `Dr Example`, `Fictional Example Lab`, `Example display DX-27` (a fictional display model; replaced a real model name on 2026-11-10, audit B11), `Example AI Inc.` are placeholders flagged `data-synthetic` in mocks; never ship them as defaults.

## 1. Tone rules

1. **Declarative, present tense, one idea per sentence.** State what is true now and what happens next: `Answer saved. Press U to reveal.` Never a question as a status (`Are you sure?`), never an exclamation mark.
2. **Honest about pixels.** The viewer never says "loading", never shows a spinner over tissue; it says what the pixels are (`Sharpening…`, `Soft — slow link`, `Offline — cached tiles only`) and names the reason after an em dash.
3. **Name the thing, then the consequence, then the action.** `3 tiles could not be loaded — retrying` · `Dx mode unavailable: this display cannot be attested. Viewing in Nav mode.` · `Metered connection — case download paused` + `Download anyway`.
4. **The viewer never diagnoses.** It hands back evidence; the diagnosis is dictated in the LIS. Copy says `Request sign-out`, `hand status and evidence back`, `Preliminary diagnosis … Clinical-IH only`, never `Sign out`, `Diagnose`, `Report`. Consult surfaces always carry `Consultation · preliminary only`.
5. **No blame, no apology, no mascot.** Not `Oops`, not `Sorry`, not `Something went wrong`. A failure names its cause and the next step (`Could not post to gradebook — your answer is saved; we will retry.`).
6. **Numbers carry units; magnification carries its derivation.** `20×` is always within reach of `0.50 µm/px · pitch EDID` or `· derived`; a tilde (`~20×`) and `Pitch unknown` whenever the pitch is not attested. Measurements are µm/mm from MPP, never pixels.
7. **Teach the key in place.** Every control's label or accessible name carries its shortcut; hints read `Press N` / `⇧↵ opens…`; the command echo prints command names, not keys.
8. **Regulatory strings are tokens.** They are quoted verbatim, never paraphrased, truncated, restyled or localised in the badge (English regulatory text is also shown in every locale's About, principles §2.5 rule 6).
9. **Learner copy is encouraging without grading by tone.** Feedback is glyph + fact (`Correct · B · 1 / 1`), confidence "feeds your review queue, not your grade", and half credit is explained by the rule stated on the card. Residents are told exactly what is hidden and when it unlocks.
10. **Research copy is precise about provenance and identity.** Producer, version, inputHash, date, regulatoryStatus; `byte-identical`; `tiling locally so zoom stays smooth`; QuPath names verbatim and the RGB restoration rule.
11. **Status strip: one notice at a time, chips for the rest.** Chips are noun phrases (`Tier 2 · 60 Hz`), the notice a sentence or sentence fragment, the live region speaks it unchanged.
12. **Accessible names are full sentences where the visual is a glyph.** `Pan up`, `Fly there: region C at 20 times`, `Metastasis detector, hidden`, `Render mode DX. Opens the Dx gate (Shift+D)`.
