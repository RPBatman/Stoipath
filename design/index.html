Concepts and judging nine wireframes · three judges · six weighted criteria

For each screen, three deliberately different angles on the same brief and content: A Canvas-maximal (chrome collapses to the edges, keyboard-summoned drawers, radial at the pointer), B Workbench (persistent, resizable, dockable panels; palette as the universal entry) and C Task-flow (a step rail organises the chrome around the task sequence; pen and touch first). A practising pathologist/educator, a UX and accessibility specialist and a front-end/engine engineer scored each 1–10 on workflow fit (0.30), clarity (0.20), accessibility (0.15), feasibility (0.15), device fit (0.15) and distinctiveness (0.05). The winner became the base of the final; the others donated mechanisms.

What every screen is held to hard constraints from the plan

  • The slide canvas dominates: ≥ 70 % of the viewport at rest on every surface; nothing covers the central 50 % × 50 %, dialogs included; chrome never re-lays out during a gesture.
  • Product and mode unmistakable: badge, full label string and NAV/DX indicator fixed in the top bar in every state; four builds, one engine; the RUO build refuses clinical launches.
  • Identity before pixels: accession, part, block, label text and the label image before the first tile in the clinical builds; a mismatch blocks.
  • Keyboard-first sign-out, pen and touch first on tablets; wheel and pinch anchored at the pointer; every drag has a single-pointer alternative; 24 px targets, 44 px under a coarse pointer.
  • Neutral chrome, safe palette: no saturated colour within 32 px of tissue; status by luminance, shape and text; annotation colours pairwise distinguishable under CVD simulation and on H&E.
  • Micrometres only: MPP-aware scale bar, derived magnification with the pitch source, ~20× and Pitch unknown when not attested.
  • Degraded states are designed: cold 4G, offline pack, W60, missing attestation, tile errors, slow translator each have a named state and fixed microcopy; never a blank canvas.
  • Launch contexts: AP-LIS launch and EHR iframe (no popups, no top navigation, postMessage), LTI 1.3 cookieless iframe, standalone, Jupyter widget, QuPath GeoJSON in and out.
