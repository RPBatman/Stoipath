        Design decisions · final education module · what was taken from which concept, where the judges disagreed, and the engine-contract map

        Final design, 2026-11-01, synthesised from the three judged concepts (C 8.03 · B 6.98 · A 6.67, weighted). The plan (docs/plan/12 §5, 02 FR-E1–E12 and NFR-A1–A5, 01 §3) and design/principles-and-ia.md are the sources of truth; departures are listed as Design notes at the end. Every element the engine or an integration would drive carries data-live (toggle Live markers in the demo strip). The product switch in the demo strip is review-only: each product is a separate build (plan 12 §2), the flow shell is a shared component library.

        1. The shell: concept C (Task-flow), kept and corrected

        • Kept from C: the phase rail Open → Screen → Examine → Decide → Complete that discloses tools per phase (with All tools and the palette as the expert escape hatches); one task card with one 56 px primary action bound to ↵ and a smaller secondary row; card header stating graded / practice / CME post-test / ungraded unknown and the LMS destination; the readiness check before the first tile; low-power coverage during Screen; the honest zoom gate (highest magnification reached over the focus) with the half-credit rule and 5 to jump to 20×; Mark without a pointer as a visible button and a rail tool; confidence that feeds the review queue, not the grade, with the calibration line at reveal; reveal as a layer-ACL gate refused while a tile under the mark is stale; the review queue only in the Complete phase; Fly to what you missed; the resident unknown with a button-reordered differential (SC 2.5.7) and "Diagnosis recorded 06:12 into the case"; record-a-tour with VIEW / INK / MIC and pause-cuts-a-step; the LMS host frame around every education mock; focus to the task h2 on a step change; the Dx-refused clinical variant proving the shell does not fork.
        • Corrected (judges' measured misses): the step rail is 64 px (80 at 4K) instead of 84 and the card 320 px (440 at 4K), which lifts the canvas at 1920 × 1080 from 70.3 % to 73.2 % with the LMS frame on (P1 now has margin, see the table in §6); phase buttons and navigator pan buttons are 44 px under a coarse pointer; the sheet peek shows the stem as well as the title and the CTA; the expanded sheet is capped at 40 % of the viewport on tablets and phones (body scrolls) and landscape uses the side drawer, so no sheet ever enters the central zone; the CTA follows the content on pointer surfaces (≥ 1600 px) instead of sitting at the foot of an empty 400 px column at 4K, and stays pinned at the foot of touch sheets where the thumb needs it; the tissue mock is a seeded, non-lattice H&E with lymphocyte-dense cortex, follicles with mantle and germinal centre, sinuses with histiocytes, a fibrous capsule and a cohesive nest of large pale tumour cells, so a 20× field reads as tissue, with a DAB-brown CK AE1/AE3 recolour for A1-2.

        7. Design notes for the plan owner

        • DF-1 · Reveal key. Principles §4.5 binds ⇧R to rotate anticlockwise (map A) and to reveal (Learn additions). This screen binds reveal to U and leaves ⇧R to rotation; the Learn additions become N, ⇧N, Space, ⇧↵, U, ⇧E, K, ⇧K. Please amend §4.5.
        • DF-2 · Region C. The IA names region C the tool rail; here it is a step rail that discloses tools per phase (C's DN-C1), 64 px wide (80 at 4K). Principles §3.1 should say "step / tool rail" and the module schema gains a phase-aware tool allowlist that the command registry honours (engineering judge).
        • DF-3 · Per-ROI settle query. "Never reveal while a tile under the mark is stale" needs geometry.settled(roi) or an equivalent from the quality record; file against the core backlog with an FR- id before the item engine relies on it.
        • DF-4 · Tier chip in Learn. Shown as information on the iPad (brief §10.3) although it limits no mode in Learn; owner to confirm whether Learn shows it at all.
        • DF-5 · Design-system tokens. Resolved on 2026-11-01: tokens.css now carries --color-hud-backdrop 0.80 and base.css the always-visible 44 px navigator pads (28 px visible pad on the frame), the 0.7 settled-label rule and the 24 px segmented items; this page's copies are deleted (§8). Still page-level: HUD secondary lines at 12 px.
        • DF-9 · Reduced-motion class twin. base.css mirrors the (pointer: coarse) tokens under .is-coarse, which the demo switch now uses; there is no .is-reduced-motion twin of the prefers-reduced-motion block, so the demo switch still zeroes the five motion tokens itself. One class in tokens.css would remove the last re-declaration.
        • DF-10 · Shared rules still carried per page. .palette-layer (dock-column anchor ≥ 1080 px, bottom sheet below) exists in all three finals; the components sheet's candidate rows (components.html) still nest "Fly there" inside role="radio" and should take the native-radio markup used here (§8).
        • DF-6 · Sheet rule. Base.css allows a 60 vh sheet on phones; this screen caps every sheet at 40 % and lets the body scroll. If the owner prefers taller phone sheets, principles §3.1 and base.css should agree on one number.
        • DF-7 · "Submit to your course". As C's DN-C4: AGS posts each item on itemAnswered; the last step is a completion marker, and the card says so.
        • DF-8 · Mock liberties. Tissue is a seeded SVG; "soft" is a CSS blur standing in for a magnified ancestor; marks, labels, pins and the scale bar are DOM here but GPU layers in the product (principles D11); the CVD remap is a Machado deutan matrix, not the Dalton 2026 LUT.
