        The screen in one paragraph

        Concept C's five-step reading order (Open → Screen → Examine → Annotate → Sign-out) is the structure: a 72 px step rail with that step's tools, one dock panel per step, N/⇧N to move, ⇧G to collapse the flow into plain tabs for an attending. Three things from Concept B make it safe and cheap on the chrome: a layout policy that decides per surface whether the panel starts open (4K, 1920) or closed (laptop, iPad) and says so in the dock header; the Dx gate as a six-row checklist with the verbatim refusal string; and the Sign-out step as a live hand-off section that keeps updating "A2-1 not opened" while screening continues, with focus landing on Open A2-1 first. Two things from Concept A make the at-rest state fast for an expert: when the panel is closed the navigator becomes the corner cluster (viewport, label image, three slide thumbnails with viewed state, five summon tabs with their keys), and a command echo under the identity line teaches the keymap by use. Everything dispatches through one command registry that also renders the palette and the keyboard map. Dialogs are anchored over the dock or rail columns and never the canvas centre.

        Fixes applied across all three concepts before synthesis

        • 24 px minimum on every segmented control and strip button; 44 px rail tools, cluster tabs and dock controls under pointer: coarse (iPad landscape included).
        • 4.5:1 for layer names when the layer is off: secondary text, no opacity; the word "hidden" is appended in text.
        • The lab name is in the Clinical-IH label string ("Fictional Example Lab", a build-time constant in the token).
        • Identity mismatch, session expired, manifest 503, tile errors, slow translator, GPU context loss and Dx dropped each have a rendered state using the principles §5 microcopy verbatim.
        • The status strip carries one notice plus chips, never two notices; the offline pack is a chip.
        • HUD text is the only per-frame DOM write (paintView); preset state, aria labels, trail, tray and checklist update at settle (renderSettled).
        • Focus: canvas before the rail in DOM order; dialogs trap focus and return it to the canvas; the keymap opens on its filter; the sign-out dialog opens on Open A2-1 first when a slide is unviewed.
        • Phone: flow-bar step chips 24 px (44 px under a coarse pointer); the dock sheet is capped at 40 % everywhere; the phone shows NAV, ~ magnification and Pitch unknown.
        • Preset keys fly to the exact µm/px and the pressed state is written at settle, so a preset never lands "mid-animation".

        Design notes and conflicts to file

        • DN-F1 New bindings N / ⇧N (steps; free in the clinical builds), ⇧G (guided flow), K (panel; D stays WASD pan-right) and ⇧Q (legend, already in map B) must be filed against the command registry (principles §4.5 rule 7, D15). B's U / ⇧U / ⇧P are not used.
        • DN-F2 Scale bar: at 20× (0.50 µm/px, DPR 1) a 200 µm bar is 400 CSS px, outside the 80–200 px snap rule (§5.2), so this screen shows 100 µm at 20× and 200 µm at 10× (DN-A1 / DN-C2). The brief's example pairing cannot hold under the plan's rule; the owner should confirm which rule wins.
        • DN-F3 Clinical context from the LIS (narrative, prior accession, specimen parts with scanning status) and the frozen-section timestamps need a read-only integration-service contract outside the launch-context contract (plan 04 §8 / plan 12 §1.1) and carry PHI; file with an FR- id before any pillar relies on them.
        • DN-F4 Layout policy table (principles §7 made explicit): panel open at ≥ 1600 px; closed at 1080–1599 and on touch surfaces; the dock header states the decision. Replaces DN-C4 (drawer open on step entry) and B's runtime governor (DN-B2).
        • DN-F5 On touch surfaces the dock sheet is capped at 40 % of the viewport and, since the sweep of 2026-11-01, dialog sheets too (--layout-sheet-max; they were 50 %), with internal scroll; both are closed by default. While either is open it covers the lower part of the central 50 % × 50 % zone on a portrait tablet or phone (a sheet taller than 25 % of the canvas cannot avoid it); the sheet is transient and user-opened, and phones are not a sign-out surface. The headless audit records these as the only central-zone intrusions; since the revision of 2026-11-01 the bottom HUD lifts above the open sheet (P8), which on a 390 px phone places the magnification and scale bar inside the central zone while the sheet is open (not on a portrait tablet, where they clear it horizontally).
        • DN-F6 A's radial tool is kept as a post-MVP, static-time, DOM-only option; this screen ships the plan's right-click / long-press context menu (§4.1) instead.
        • DN-F7 The frozen-consult variant keeps the Tier-2 label image on an in-lab iPad under MDM (role-scoped); a share-link consult is Tier 0 and shows none (DN-C3). The purpose strings are the plan's four; "Frozen · in review" as a worklist state is a proposal (DN-B5).
        • DN-F8 The preset ladder follows the plan (1.25 / 2.5 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 40×, derived, D8); the brief's "4×" is reachable by typed zoom.
        • DN-F9 Selecting the RUO product shows the launch-refused plate rather than a research workspace: the clinical launch has no home in that build (plan 04 §8, FR-R3).
