Design rationale · Concept A · Canvas-maximal · what the judges should look at

Thesis

The research pathologist and the ML engineer use this screen for hours a day, on a 4K display, with one hand on the mouse. Every surveyed viewer gives them a ribbon, a rail and a dock that sit there all day even though they are touched a few times an hour. Concept A keeps only what principle P4 and P3 make mandatory (the top bar with the RUO badge and string, the status strip with the honest sharpness indicator) and turns everything else into a transient: an edge drawer that a single key summons (I cohort, K layers, ⇧K annotations, U model, ⇧E export), a tool wheel at the pointer (right-click, long-press, pen squeeze, ⇧V), a class legend that lives in the HUD under the navigator (⇧Q), and a top-left HUD tool line that says what the next click will do. The canvas share at rest is ≈ 93 % at 1920 × 1080 and ≈ 96 % at 3840 × 2160; with the drawer open it is still ≈ 74 %.

Key choices

  1. One drawer, five tabs, one key each. The drawer is the design system's dock element positioned over the canvas edge (never the centre) between gestures; pressing its key again or Esc closes it. On tablets it is the bottom sheet (≤ 40 % height), on phones a 60 % sheet. Because it is the same element, ` can pin it (plus the rail) and the screen becomes the classic three-column layout: the discoverability escape hatch costs no second implementation.
  2. The HUD is the toolbar. The top-left line shows the active tool with its key, the active class with its swatch, undo depth and the LOD state of the dense layer. Both the tool and the class are buttons (24 px targets) that open the wheel and the class ring, so a pointer-only user is never stranded, and the line is updated by textContent only (P2).
  3. Tool wheel instead of a rail. Eight single-letter tools around a centre that shows the class (Q turns the ring into the class picker, 1–9 choose). It is pointer sugar over the command bus: every sector is also a key, the palette lists every command, so WCAG keyboard operability holds without the wheel. In the product it is a user-invoked GPU layer (exempt from the central-50 % rule like the pen-tip palette).
  4. Legend as HUD, counts as data. The class legend sits under the navigator, outside the central 50 %, and carries the annotation counts per class plus the detection layer's class totals (1,240,000) and its LOD; clicking a class jumps to its first object (Slim convention). It is the research user's answer to "what is on this slide" without a panel.
  5. Compare and multiplex are canvas modes, not panels. C splits the canvas (active pane outlined, QuPath convention) with a compare bar at the top edge for lock / affine / none sync, / turns it into a curtain with a keyboard-operable handle (SC 2.5.7), and ⇧G fans the multiplex slide into one pane per enabled channel.
  6. Honesty is in the strip, not the panels. The strip carries the sharpness state (Sharp / Sharpening… / Soft — level not yet available), the local-tiler notice, the network, offline and tier chips and the Dx refusal strings verbatim from principles §5. The canvas is softened, never blanked, in the degraded states; failed tiles keep the ancestor.
  7. Jupyter is the same chrome, smaller. The widget surface moves the same app node into a notebook output cell: 36 px bar with the RUO badge, drawers still summoned by keys, 24 px strip with Syncing with Python…. Nothing is re-implemented in Python.

Trade-offs and the discoverability risk, honestly

  • Discoverability is the price. A first-time user sees a slide and a top bar. Mitigations shown here: the HUD hint line (shown until the first command, telemetry commandInvoked decides when it has done its job), the ? sheet, Ctrl+K, the ⋯ menu listing every panel with its key, the "Pin chrome" button in the top bar and inside every drawer, and the touch bottom bar which always shows five labelled targets. Whether that is enough is exactly what a formative study with 6–8 research users should test before this concept is chosen.
  • Five new bindings (K, ⇧K, U, ⇧E, ⇧G, ⇧V, `) are proposed as Design note D-A1 against the command registry; all are unmodified or Shift keys, so the Hyperdrive rule holds, and all are rebindable. I is reused from the clinical identity panel because the question it answers ("what am I looking at") is the same.
  • The drawer covers the right 360 px of tissue while open. The alternative (push the canvas) would re-lay out the canvas, which P2 forbids during a gesture and which costs a frame between gestures; the concept accepts the occlusion because the drawer is transient. Pinning restores the push layout for people who prefer it.
  • Hover is not used for anything except pan buttons in the navigator; nothing auto-fades, nothing slides. That is a deliberate loss of "polish" in exchange for P2 and for pen/touch parity.

What to look at (switch states from the demo strip or the URL hash)

  • At rest at 1920 × 1080: badge, full RUO string, cohort name with CC0, HUD tool line, class legend, strip notice 1,240,000 features — tiling locally so zoom stays smooth, scale bar, derived magnification, navigator. Toggle "Central 50 %" to verify nothing crosses it.
  • Press K: the layers drawer with the AI raster (opacity, outline/heatmap, threshold, provenance tuple with regulatoryStatus: research), the hidden label raster, the 1.24 M vector layer with the local-tiler note, and the annotation set.
  • Press ⇧K: the annotation list is a real list with counts; the class chips carry QuPath names on the CVD-safe palette; the deuteranopia toggle re-colours the overlays only (Machado 2009 matrix).
  • Right-click the slide (or ⇧V): the tool wheel; Q inside it shows the class ring.
  • Slide → "Compare" and "Curtain": affine sync, active pane outline, curtain handle operable by arrow keys.
  • Slide → "CyCIF 8-channel": six of eight channels, per-channel colour and window with histogram, the 8-bit preview tier while moving · 16-bit at settle line; with tier W60 the fifth and sixth channels explain the cap. "Channel grid" shows ⇧G.
  • Surface → "Jupyter widget": compact chrome in a notebook cell with Syncing with Python….
  • Network → Cold 4G, Offline pack, Tile errors, Translator slow; sharpness → Soft — level not yet available; build → Clinical-IH with dx → Refused: the exact §5 strings in the strip and the live region.
  • Resize to 1024 × 1366 (bottom bar, sheet, pen stroke with a finger pan) and 390 × 844 (badge only, sharpness dot in the HUD, navigator behind O, point + measure).

Design notes (choices the plan leaves open; flagged, not decided)

  • D-A1 Panel keys. K, ⇧K, U, ⇧E, ⇧G, ⇧V, ` are free in principles §4.5 maps A and B and are proposed as registry additions. The component sheet labels the rail's Layers tool ⇧H while map A binds ⇧H to "toggle AI overlay only"; this concept keeps map A and uses K for the panel. Owner to confirm.
  • D-A2 The wheel is not a mode. Right-click on an annotation still opens the design system's context menu; the wheel appears on empty canvas, on long-press and on pen squeeze. Both are transient GPU layers in the product.
  • D-A3 Drawer width. 360 px (440 on 4K) rather than the dock's 320: research rows (provenance tuple, window histogram) need the width. Resizable 320–520 by keyboard as the dock is.
  • D-A4 Compare bar placement. Top edge of the canvas, horizontally centred, which is outside the central 50 % × 50 % zone but inside the top 25 %. If the owner prefers zero DOM in the top band, the control moves into the strip.
  • D-A5 Multichannel pseudo-colours. Six of the eight channel colours are palette tokens; CD68 (#4f7ddb) and PD-L1 (#c2673a) are additional LUT colours because deep blue and sienna vanish on a black background. Pseudo-colours are user-set LUTs, not the annotation palette, but the same ΔE00 warning should apply.
  • D-A7 Label string at 1024 px. base.css hides .topbar__label through @container topbar (max-width: 1023px), which measures the top bar's content box (viewport − 16 px), so the string disappears at exactly 1024 px, against principles §2.5. This page overrides it; the design system should query max-width: 1007px or drop the padding from the container. Flagged for the design-system owner.
  • D-A6 First-run hint. A HUD hint line until the first command. It is DOM text over the canvas (top-left corner, allowed), never a modal or a coach-mark tour.

Known weaknesses

  • Novices may never find the drawers without the hint; the escape hatch (pin chrome) is itself behind a small button and a key.
  • The drawer occludes the right edge of the slide while open; a reader who keeps the layers panel open all day is better served by the pinned layout, i.e. by the other concepts.
  • Legend and navigator stack on the right edge: on a 1366 × 1024 landscape tablet with the compare bar visible the top band gets crowded (the bar is left-aligned there to compensate).
  • The tool wheel's keyboard twin is "the keys themselves"; a screen-reader user never needs the wheel, but a motor-impaired pointer user who wants a bigger target gets the pinned rail only.
  • On tablet and phone the bottom sheet covers the bottom HUD (scale bar, magnification) while open; the values stay in the live region, but a visual twin above the sheet is still to be designed.
  • Static mock: zoom is a transform of a synthetic SVG; the detection layer is 1,600 drawn centroids standing in for 1.24 M; the 8-bit/16-bit tier switch is text only.

Content is synthetic: CAMELYON16 slide names are real public identifiers (CC0); the IHC serial section, the CyCIF field, model names and hashes are placeholders. No PHI appears in this build by construction.
